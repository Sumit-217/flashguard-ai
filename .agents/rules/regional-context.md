# Regional Context Rule: Uttarakhand, India

All data, references, mock datasets, IoT telemetry, geospatial coordinates, and scenarios in the FlashGuard AI project MUST be grounded in **Uttarakhand, India**.

## Mandatory Regional Guidelines

1. **Target Geography**:
   - State: **Uttarakhand, India**
   - Districts: Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Uttarkashi, Dehradun, Pauri Garhwal, Tehri Garhwal, Pithoragarh.
   - Key Locations:
     - **Joshimath** (Chamoli): `30.5564° N, 79.5678° E` (Primary demo site for flood/land subsidence/GLOF).
     - **Kedarnath / Gaurikund** (Rudraprayag): `30.7346° N, 79.0669° E` (Flash flood, cloudburst).
     - **Dharasu** (Uttarkashi): `30.6322° N, 78.3186° E` (Landslide, highway blockage).
     - **Rishikesh / Haridwar** (Dehradun/Haridwar): `30.0869° N, 78.2676° E` (Downstream flood monitoring).

2. **Authorities & Benchmarks**:
   - Uttarakhand State Disaster Management Authority (USDMA)
   - State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) Uttarakhand
   - India Meteorological Department (IMD) Meteorological Centre Dehradun

3. **Disaster Typologies**:
   - Himalayan flash floods & GLOF (Glacial Lake Outburst Floods)
   - Cloudbursts & intense monsoon rainfall (>64.5 mm / 24h)
   - Landslides along NH-58 / NH-107 Char Dham highway corridors
   - River water level surges along Alaknanda, Mandakini, Bhagirathi, and Ganga rivers.
